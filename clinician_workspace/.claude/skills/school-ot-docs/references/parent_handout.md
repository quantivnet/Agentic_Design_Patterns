# Format: Parent Handout

One page, roughly 6th-grade reading level: short sentences, everyday words, no clinical jargon (explain any unavoidable term in parentheses). Warm and encouraging. Never state or imply a medical diagnosis.

Structure (title: "Helping Your Child at Home"):

- **What We're Working On** — 2–3 sentences, plain language
- **Activities to Try at Home** — 3–5 activities; for each: what to do, why it helps, how often
- **Safety Notes**
- **What Progress Looks Like** — realistic expectations and timeframe, plain language
- **Questions?** — one line inviting contact with the school OT
- **Information We Still Need** — omit only if there are no gaps

No blocking intake requirements; warn if parent concerns are undocumented (the handout can't address them directly).
