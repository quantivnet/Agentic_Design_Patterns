# Format: IEP Evaluation Section

Professional clinical language for an educational evaluation team. Sections, in this exact order:

1. **Background and Reason for Referral**
2. **Assessment Methods and Dates**
3. **Functional Findings: Fine Motor** — narrative paragraph, then bulleted "Assessment Results"
4. **Functional Findings: Sensory Processing** — narrative paragraph, then bulleted "Assessment Results"
5. **Clinical Interpretation** — synthesize findings into educational impact; no medical diagnoses
6. **Goals** — per identified area of need: 2–3 ranked SMART alternates with one-line rationales; distinguish short-term objectives from long-term/annual goals
7. **Recommended Services** — frequency, duration, location/model, justification
8. **Accommodations and Adaptive Equipment** — specific items with specs, rationale, expected benefit
9. **Classroom Strategies**
10. **Home Activity Suggestions**
11. **Follow-Up Plan and Referrals**
12. **Missing Information / Assumptions**

Blocking intake requirements for this format: a documented reason for referral, and at least one source of assessment data (standardized test, observation, or functional performance).
